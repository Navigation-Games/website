# Meet the Team

There have been too many people involved in Navigation Games over the years to name everyone - we have had hundreds of volunteers and part-timers who helped deliver programs, ran the organization and provided input into everything that we do. A special shout out to our amazing technology team, who are working on EasyGec to make it more user friendly for us and our partners.

### Erkan Sezgin

- Role: Program Staff
- Photo: erkan-sezgin.jpg
- Email: erkan@navigationgames.org

Erkan was born and raised in Turkey, and after traveling the world, he eventually found his way to Boston, guided not by an ordinary compass, but one that follows the heart, as he likes to say.

He discovered orienteering while serving in the Turkish Air Force, and it quickly became more than a sport - it became a way of life. He spent years on Turkey’s national team and coached athletes who competed internationally.

Through schools, camps, and outdoor clubs, Erkan has introduced countless people to the thrill of map and compass navigation. Along the way, he’s shared his culture, explored others, and built bridges across communities.

Now, with Navigation Games, he’s helping the next generation discover that sometimes, getting a little lost in the woods is the best way to find yourself.

### Barb Bryant

- Role: President
- Photo: barb-bryant.jpg
- Email: president@navigationgames.org

Barb got started by volunteering in her kids' classrooms in Cambridge, MA, and one thing led to another. She is now the President of Navigation Games and her focus is to bring orienteering into schools and to every child in the city. Barb is also the former Vice President of Youth Initiatives for Orienteering USA. Barb believes that orienteering teaches kids how to make good decisions: to think and act at the same time in a concerted way.

Certifications: Safesport Certified, First Aid/CPR/AED Certified
